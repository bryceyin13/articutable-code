- the supplied recognition manifest is authoritative for object IDs, exact category strings, identities, operability_candidate, reconstruction-unit boundaries, and component ownership
- do not silently add, remove, split, merge, duplicate, or rename recognized objects

Required internal analysis order — highest priority:
1. Load the recognition manifest and preserve its object identities and boundaries.
2. Analyze per-object dimensions, placement, orientation, appearance, operability details, rigid parts, joint, motion range, and physical support relation.
3. Check geometric and articulation consistency against both images without redoing object recognition.

Direct-support policy:

- support_parents must be a nonempty array of unique references to table_0 or other object_ids, never the object itself, and every support chain must end at table_0
